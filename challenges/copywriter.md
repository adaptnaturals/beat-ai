## About This Challenge

At Adapt Naturals, we live by "test, don't guess" — including in hiring.

This audition works differently than most, in two ways.

**First: you're not competing against other candidates. You're competing against our AI.** These exact briefs have already been completed by **Jarvis, our in-house AI content system** — built on Claude, writing in our founder's corpus-derived voice, with automated compliance review. Its output is frozen as the baseline. A committee of four Adapt team members will review your work and Jarvis's blind — same template, no names — and you advance only if at least three of the four score your work higher. **A 2–2 split is a tie, and ties don't advance.**

We're honest about why: Jarvis already writes our emails. The only reason to hire a copywriter is work that's *better* than what we already produce. This audition tests for exactly that — which is also why it's paid, and why the bar is high.

**Second: this is real.** The two campaign emails will actually be sent — your version against Jarvis's version in a live A/B test to our list. Revenue per recipient, click-through, and unsubscribe rates will be measured. The committee's blind verdict decides the hire; the live results validate it. If your copy wins, it ships under our name to tens of thousands of subscribers — that's what the fee and the permission agreement cover.

## The Assignment

Three real briefs, delivered as separate files. They are complete — audience, offer, structure, non-negotiables, and a verified research appendix. **Fill them in place.** The briefs are the spec; read every line before you write, especially the non-negotiables. Jarvis received these identical files and nothing more.

1. **Brief 1 — Luminous Mind: Founder/Customer Story** (campaign). Two variants of one email: V1 told by Chris, V2 told through a customer. This is the heaviest deliverable — treat it that way.
2. **Brief 2 — Luminous Mind: Product Deep Dive + Testimonials** (campaign). The rational counterpart. Mechanism-first, dose-forward, honesty as strategy.
3. **Brief 3 — Post-Purchase Day 16: "Why You Started"** (flow). Write these three product variants only: **Luminous Mind, Steady Spirit, and the Dynamic Fallback** — plus all non-variant sections. (The full flow has twelve variants; the winner completes the rest as their first assignment.)

Everything you need is in the briefs and their appendices. **Do not add facts that are not there.** If a section needs information you don't have, that's worth noticing.

## What We're Evaluating

| Criteria | Weight |
|----------|--------|
| Brief 1 — Story email, both variants | 40% |
| Brief 2 — Deep dive | 30% |
| Brief 3 — Day 16 flow (3 variants) | 20% |
| AI workflow answer + Loom | 10% |

Each deliverable is scored blind on voice fidelity, brief compliance, claims safety, persuasion craft, and judgment. Compliance is a gate, not a dimension: an email that makes a disease claim, invents a fact, or rewrites a customer's words cannot be sent — and an email that can't be sent can't win.

**Specifically, we're looking for:**

- Chris's voice held for three emails straight — calm, precise, zero hype
- The discipline to follow a tight brief *and* the judgment to know when a brief is telling you something between the lines
- Honesty deployed as persuasion — concessions that build trust instead of dampening intent
- Compliance fluency — structure/function language that still sells
- Copy that would beat a very good AI on a blind read, because that's precisely what it has to do

**What we're NOT looking for:**

- DTC swipe-file energy — urgency stacks, open-loop subject lines, "last chance"
- Copy that could sell any supplement — the briefs are full of specifics; use them
- Improved customer quotes, invented founder details, or filled-in gaps
- Perfect compliance with no pulse. The voice is calm, not lifeless.
