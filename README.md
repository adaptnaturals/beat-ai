# Beat the AI — Adapt Naturals Hiring Challenges

At Adapt Naturals, we live by **"test, don't guess"** — including in hiring.

Every role we hire for gets an audition challenge that mirrors the real work. Before any candidate sees it, we give the identical brief — same data, same instructions, same time budget — to **Claude, Anthropic's AI**. Claude's output becomes the baseline.

**To advance, you have to beat it.**

## How it works

1. We design a challenge that mirrors the first 30 days in the seat, including a data packet when the role calls for one.
2. Claude completes the challenge first. Its submission is frozen as the baseline.
3. Candidates complete the same challenge. **AI use is not just allowed — it's expected.** We're scoring your thinking, not your typing.
4. Three reviewers score each submission blind against Claude's baseline. Majority decision stands. **Ties don't advance.**

## Why we do this

AI can produce competent, well-structured marketing work from a clean brief in minutes. If your work isn't better than that, the honest question is why we'd hire it. But when someone *does* beat the baseline — sharper judgment, better prioritization, catches the things the model missed — that difference is exactly what we're paying for. This process finds it faster than any resume screen.

It also means the tedious parts of hiring get more honest in both directions: you see exactly what the job is before you take it, and we see exactly how you work before we offer it.

## Ground rules

- All account figures in challenge data packets are **illustrative** — modeled to behave like the real thing, but fictional. Publicly available facts about Adapt Naturals are genuine.
- Use any tools you want, including AI. Show your work where the brief asks for it.
- Candidate submissions are used for evaluation only and are never used commercially — unless the challenge is a **paid audition** (marked in the brief), in which case the fee covers agreed usage, such as live A/B testing the work against the AI baseline.
