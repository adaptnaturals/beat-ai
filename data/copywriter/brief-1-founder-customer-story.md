## Non-negotiables from Chris

- **Voice: calm clinician explaining something to a smart adult.** No emoji, no exclamation marks, no hype. If a line would sound at home in a nootropics Reddit thread, cut it.  
- **This is a story email, not a sales email with a story on top.** The formula does not appear until section 05\. Resist naming ingredients early.  
- **Never a disease claim.** Not memory loss, not cognitive decline, not dementia, not ADHD, not long COVID, not depression. Write about focus, clarity, recall, mental energy, mood, motivation. Structure/function only.  
- **Timeline guardrail.** Alpinia galanga (enXtra) is genuinely fast — published work supports improved alertness within about an hour. The rest of the formula compounds; the site's own FAQ says two to three months for full effects. Say both. **Never promise a date, and never tell the reader they must wait for the good part.**  
- **Do not imply the reader is broken.** The reader is a capable adult whose brain is under more load than it was built for. That is the frame.  
- **Do not position against a diagnosis or against ageing as decline.** Position against the modern load: digital distraction, chronic stress, nutrient-poor food, caffeine dependence.  
- **No invented biography.** Every fact about Chris must come from the Appendix or be supplied by Chris directly. If a beat needs a detail we do not have, flag it in the margin — do not fill the gap.  
- **Customer quotes are verbatim.** Trim for length only. Never rewrite a customer's words, never merge two reviews into one, never improve a sentence.

**Positioning:** Cognitive Enhancing Brain Booster. 60 capsules, 30-day supply. Caffeine-free, stimulant-free, non-habit-forming.

**Approved benefit language (from the live page):** boost focus, memory, and alertness · enhance mental clarity, creativity, and positivity · elevate mood and boost motivation · protect and nourish brain cells · improve blood flow to the brain.

**Four mechanisms:**

1. **Focus, memory, processing speed**: Bacopa, lion's mane, citicoline working synergistically on attention, recall, processing.  
2. **Protection against age-related change**: phosphatidylserine and ginkgo supporting neuronal health.  
3. **Neurotransmitter optimisation**: uridine and citicoline supply raw materials for acetylcholine and dopamine synthesis, supporting mood, motivation, mental energy.  
4. **Cerebral circulation**: ginkgo and bacopa support brain microcirculation, delivering glucose, oxygen, nutrients.
