## Non-negotiables from Chris

- **Voice: calm clinician explaining something to a smart adult.** No emoji, no exclamation marks, no hype. The reader is being shown the work, not sold to.  
- **Depth is the differentiator, not length.** This email can be long, but every sentence must carry information. Cut anything that is atmosphere.  
- **Mechanism before ingredient, always.** Lead each block with what the brain is doing, then name the compound that supports it. Never a list of names in search of a reason.  
- **Do not overclaim the synergy story.** "No other supplement contains this combination at these dosages" is approved brand language. "Clinically proven," "guaranteed," and "the only formula that works" are not.  
- **Never a disease claim.** Not memory loss, not cognitive decline, not dementia, not ADHD, not depression, not long COVID. Structure/function only: focus, clarity, recall, mental energy, mood, motivation.  
- **Timeline guardrail.** enXtra (Alpinia galanga) is genuinely fast — published work supports improved alertness in as little as an hour. Uridine, citicoline, and ginkgo compound and need consistency; the FAQ says two to three months for full effects. Both are true and both go in section 07\. **Never promise a date. Never suggest the reader must wait for the good part.**  
- **Testimonials are verbatim.** Trim for length only. Never rewrite, never merge, never improve a customer's sentence.  
- **Handle the ginkgo caution properly.** It is not a liability we hide; it is evidence the doses are real. Link it, don't bury it.
