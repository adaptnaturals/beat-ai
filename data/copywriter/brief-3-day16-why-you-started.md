## **Non-negotiables from Chris**

> * Do **not** establish that everyone waits until week 5–6 to notice something. Many people feel a difference sooner. Let it sit around 3 weeks  
> * Do communicate: benefits compound over time; a problem that took years to develop won’t be fully solved in two weeks; a small minority may not notice much early.  
> * Never frame the reader as being in that minority, and never suggest they must wait for the “good part.”  
> * Voice: calm clinician explaining something to a smart adult. No emoji, no exclamation marks, no hype.

## **04 · Dynamic product call-out: 11 variants**

One forest panel sits in the middle of the email and swaps by the subscriber’s primary product. Each variant needs two pieces: an **italic serif benefit statement** (what they came for, aka the outcome in their words, 12–22 words, sentence fragments allowed) and a **sixteen-days-in paragraph** (what has happened in the body so far, 30–50 words, naming the formula and at least one real ingredient or mechanism).

**Rules for every variant:** Lead with the mechanism the product is actually known for, not a single ingredient in isolation. Never promise a date for results. Never imply nothing is happening yet, just describe what *is* happening. Products with a fast-acting component should say so.
